AI Factory — canvas v2

Static mockup. Every sentence, state and number on this page is the real copy and the real shape of the data; nothing here is wired up. The question this design answers: eight blocks at a readable size do not fit on one screen.

1 · The canvas, mid-run

The answer: a fisheye along the flow. The running block and its two neighbours are full panels. Everything else collapses to an 80px spine that still carries its mark, its name, its state and its badge — so all eight blocks are always on screen, the left-to-right reading never breaks, and the full detail follows the work as it moves. Click a spine card and it becomes the panel.

Glow only on what matters. One blue card pulls the eye. Four finished blocks are green and then quiet.

Junction dots on every orthogonal cable. The flow looks engineered, not sketched.

2 · Card anatomy

Six zones. The one that matters is the third: the evidence strip holds only measured facts — test results, elapsed time, attempt, diff stat — and nothing a language model said. A card whose result is only a claim has no strip at all, so the difference between "a test passed" and "an AI said it's fine" is structural, not a colour you could miss.

1 · Identity — type mark, name at 19px, and the model chip inside the card.
2 · State — the word, always. Colour only reinforces it. Elapsed time beside it.
3 · Headline — one sentence at 16px. What happened, in plain language.
4 · Evidence — measured facts only. Twelve pips you can count without reading a number.
5 · Activity — the last three steps while it runs. A ticker, not a log.
6 · Spend — tokens and cost, or the words "Not reported". Never a zero.

3 · Every state

Running, passed, failed, waiting on a person, quiet — plus the two special cards: a block switched off, and a block added after the run started, which has no state at all because the run never heard of it.

4 · A failure, traced

Review turned the work down. The red cable leaves Approve, drops under the lane and re-enters Build — you can follow a failure by eye from where it was produced to where it stopped everything. Only the block where the failure started glows; the ones it knocked over are red and quiet, so the origin stays findable.

5 · Waiting on you

The state the whole product exists for. Amber, full panel, and the only card on screen with any light on it.

On a 1,440px laptop the fisheye keeps two full panels instead of three. The running block, or the one that needs you, is always one of them.
The decision controls (approve / turn down) land with the human gate in a later milestone. The card is designed with room for them.
